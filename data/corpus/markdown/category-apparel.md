# Category Policy Addendum: Apparel

**Kartway Marketplace - Apparel Policy Addendum**

**1. Return Window**  
1.1 The return window for apparel items is 45 days from the date of delivery.  
1.2 Items returned after the 45-day period will be ineligible for a refund.  

**2. Category-Specific Condition Requirements**  
2.1 Apparel items must be returned in new, unworn, and unwashed condition to qualify for a refund.  
2.2 Items showing signs of wear, stains, or alterations will be rejected.  

**3. Original Packaging Requirement**  
3.1 Original packaging is required for all apparel returns.  
3.2 Items returned without original packaging will incur a deduction of 20% from the refund amount.  

**4. Restocking Fees**  
4.1 A restocking fee of $5 will be applied to all apparel returns not meeting the above-mentioned condition requirements.  
4.2 There is no restocking fee applied to items returned in compliance with the return conditions.  

**5. Hazardous or Perishable Items**  
5.1 Apparel does not fall under the hazardous or perishable category; however, items with specific care labels (e.g., items requiring dry cleaning) must be returned with care.  
5.2 Any apparel item returned that has been treated or altered should not be sent back and will be considered ineligible for a refund.  

**6. Warranty Period**  
6.1 The warranty period for apparel items is 90 days from the date of delivery, covering manufacturer defects only.  
6.2 Products deemed defective within the warranty period must be reported to customer support within 14 days of discovery for a replacement or refund.  

**End of Apparel Policy Addendum**
