# Category Policy Addendum: Sports & Outdoors

**Policy Addendum: Sports & Outdoors Category**

**1. Return Window**  
1.1 The return window for products in the Sports & Outdoors category is established as 60 days from the date of delivery.  
1.2 Products returned after this 60-day period will not be accepted, and the return request will be automatically denied.  

**2. Category-Specific Condition Requirements**  
2.1 Items returned in this category must be in new, unused condition with no signs of wear or damage.  
2.2 If an item is returned with indications of use (e.g., scuff marks, dirt, or water damage), it will be classified as "not eligible for return."  

**3. Original Packaging**  
3.1 Original packaging is mandatory for all returns in the Sports & Outdoors category.  
3.2 Products returned without the original packaging will be subject to a 15% deduction from the refund amount.  

**4. Restocking Fees**  
4.1 A restocking fee of 10% will be applied to all returned items in this category unless they are defective or incorrect.  
4.2 Requests for return on these grounds must be initiated within 30 days from delivery to avoid the restocking fee.  

**5. Hazardous or Perishable Handling Rules**  
5.1 Products classified as hazardous (e.g., certain sports chemicals) are non-returnable under any circumstances.  
5.2 Perishable items (e.g., nutrition supplements with expiry dates) must be returned within 15 days of delivery to qualify for a refund. No returns will be accepted on perishable items after this period.  

**6. Warranty Period**  
6.1 The standard warranty period for all Sports & Outdoors items is 12 months from the date of purchase unless stated otherwise in the product listing.  
6.2 Claims under warranty must provide proof of purchase and be initiated within the warranty period to be honored.  

**7. Exceptions**  
7.1 Items such as used bicycles or fitness equipment can be returned only if in original condition and within 30 days from delivery.  
7.2 Custom or personalized items are non-returnable once delivered.  

This policy addendum is effective immediately and supersedes any prior guidelines relating specifically to the Sports & Outdoors category. Compliance with these specific rules is mandatory for all Kartway support agents and may be subject to periodic review.
