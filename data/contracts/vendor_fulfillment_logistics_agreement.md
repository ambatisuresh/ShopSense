# Swiftlane Logistics Last-Mile Delivery Agreement

**Parties:** Kartway Commerce Pvt. Ltd. ("Kartway") and Swiftlane Logistics Pvt. Ltd. ("Swiftlane" or "Carrier")
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2026
**Term:** Initial term of twelve (12) months from the Effective Date.

**1.1 Scope of Services**
Swiftlane shall provide last-mile pickup and delivery services for orders dispatched from Kartway fulfillment centers to end customers across the territories listed in Exhibit A.

**2.1 Delivery Service Levels**
Swiftlane commits to the following delivery windows, measured from the time of dispatch from a Kartway fulfillment center:
(a) Metro Areas: five (5) to seven (7) business days for standard service.
(b) Non-Metro Areas: seven (7) to ten (10) business days for standard service.
(c) Express service is offered only in metro areas listed in Exhibit A, with delivery in three (3) to four (4) business days.

**3.1 Delay Compensation to Kartway**
In the event a shipment is delivered later than the applicable service level in Clause 2.1, Swiftlane shall credit Kartway a flat five percent (5%) of the shipment's carriage fee, regardless of the length of the delay.

**4.1 Lost-in-Transit Declaration**
A shipment shall be deemed lost-in-transit, and eligible for claim, only if it remains undelivered twenty-one (21) calendar days after the date of dispatch.

**5.1 Disputed Delivery Investigation**
Where Kartway or a Kartway customer disputes a delivery marked complete by Swiftlane, Swiftlane shall complete its investigation within ten (10) business days of receiving Kartway's dispute notice and shall report its findings in writing.

**6.1 Liability for Lost or Damaged Goods**
Swiftlane's liability for any lost, stolen, or damaged shipment shall not exceed USD 100.00 per package, regardless of the declared or actual value of the package's contents.

**7.1 Insurance**
Swiftlane shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of not less than USD 1,000,000 per occurrence for the duration of this Agreement.

**8.1 Termination for Convenience**
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) calendar days' prior written notice to the other party.

**9.1 Force Majeure**
Neither party shall be liable for delays or failures in performance resulting from natural disasters, severe weather, or other events classified as force majeure.
