# Shipping, Delivery and Lost-Parcel Policy

**Kartway Shipping Policy**

**1. Delivery Service Level Agreement (SLA)**  
   1.1. **Service Tiers**  
   a. **Standard Shipping**  
      i. Metro Areas: 3-5 business days  
      ii. Non-Metro Areas: 5-7 business days  
   b. **Express Shipping**  
      i. Metro Areas: 1-2 business days  
      ii. Non-Metro Areas: 3-4 business days  
   c. **Next-Day Delivery**  
      i. Available only in designated metro areas for orders placed by 2 PM local time.  
   
**2. Definition of Delayed Shipment**  
   2.1. A shipment is defined as delayed if it has not been delivered within the specified SLA outlined in Section 1.  

**3. Compensation Ladder for Delayed Shipments**  
   3.1. Compensation for delayed shipments is structured as follows:  
   a. 1-2 days delayed: 10% refund of shipping cost  
   b. 3-4 days delayed: 25% refund of shipping cost  
   c. 5 or more days delayed: 50% refund of shipping cost  
   3.2. Compensation requests must be submitted within 14 calendar days from the expected delivery date.  

**4. Lost-in-Transit Declaration Threshold**  
   4.1. A shipment will be considered lost-in-transit if it has not been delivered within 15 calendar days of the original shipping date.  

**5. Disputed Deliveries**  
   5.1. If a courier marks an order as delivered but the customer disputes the delivery, the following process will be initiated:  
   a. The customer must report the dispute within 48 hours of the marked delivery date.  
   b. Kartway will conduct an investigation, which will be completed within 5 business days.  
   c. If the investigation substantiates the customer's claim, a replacement order will be issued or a full refund will be provided, at the customer's discretion.  

**6. Address Change Cutoffs**  
   6.1. Customers may request an address change for their order up to 24 hours after placing the order.  
   6.2. Address change requests beyond the 24-hour window will not be accommodated and the original address will be used for delivery.  

**7. Exceptions**  
   7.1. Natural disasters, severe weather conditions, or any events classified as force majeure may affect delivery times, and Kartway will not be liable for delays caused by these circumstances.  

This document shall be reviewed annually and updated as necessary to reflect operational changes or to improve customer service outcomes.
