# Category Policy Addendum: Electronics

**Kartway Electronics Policy Addendum (Policy No. KWE-2023-02)**

**1. Return Window**  
1.1. Customers may return electronics items within **14 days** from the date of delivery.  
1.2. Items must be postmarked for return within this 14-day timeframe to be eligible for a refund.

**2. Category-Specific Condition Requirements**  
2.1. All returned electronics must be in **like-new condition**, meaning no visible signs of wear or damage.  
2.2. Items must be tested to confirm functionality prior to return.  
2.3. If the returned item has been modified or tampered with, the return will be denied.

**3. Original Packaging Requirement**  
3.1. Original packaging is **mandatory** for all returns of electronics items.  
3.2. Returns submitted without the original packaging will be subject to an automatic **20% restocking fee**.

**4. Restocking Fees**  
4.1. A restocking fee of **15%** will be applied to all returned electronics items unless the item is defective or was sent in error.  
4.2. The restocking fee will be calculated based on the original purchase price of the item.

**5. Hazardous or Perishable Handling Rules**  
5.1. Electronics that qualify as hazardous materials, including but not limited to batteries and certain types of lighting devices, must be returned according to **local hazardous waste regulations**.  
5.2. Perishable electronics are not acceptable for return under any circumstances.

**6. Warranty Period**  
6.1. All new electronics come with a **12-month warranty** from the date of purchase covering defects in materials and workmanship.  
6.2. Refurbished electronics hold a **6-month warranty** from the date of purchase. 

**7. Exclusions**  
7.1. The following items are not eligible for return:  
   7.1.1. Opened software products.  
   7.1.2. Items marked as "Final Sale."  
   7.1.3. Custom-built electronic devices.

**End of Policy Addendum**
