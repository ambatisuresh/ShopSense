# Category Policy Addendum: Groceries

**Kartway Marketplace: Groceries Policy Addendum**

**1. Return Window for Grocery Items**  
1.1 The return window for all grocery items is 14 days from the date of delivery.  
1.2 Any grocery item must be reported for return within this 14-day window to qualify for a refund or exchange.

**2. Category-Specific Condition Requirements**  
2.1 Grocery items must be returned in their original, unopened condition to be eligible for a full refund.  
2.2 If items are opened, they will only be eligible for a 50% refund if they have not surpassed the expiration date and are in a condition deemed resalable by the customer operations team.  
2.3 Items that show signs of spoilage or have exceeded their expiration date are not eligible for return under any circumstances.

**3. Original Packaging Requirements**  
3.1 Original packaging is mandatory for all grocery returns.  
3.2 Items returned without original packaging will not be processed and will be returned to the customer at their expense.

**4. Restocking Fees**  
4.1 A restocking fee of $5.00 will be applied to any grocery item return that is not in its original, unopened condition.  
4.2 There will be no restocking fee applied to grocery items returned within the specified return window and in original condition.

**5. Hazardous and Perishable Handling Rules**  
5.1 Items classified as hazardous (e.g., certain cleaning products) must be returned using a carrier that complies with hazardous materials regulations within 7 days of delivery.  
5.2 Perishable items, including but not limited to fresh produce, dairy, and meat products, must be returned within 24 hours of delivery to ensure quality and safety for processing.

**6. Warranty Period**  
6.1 The warranty period for grocery items is 30 days from the date of delivery against defects in quality and workmanship, excluding perishables which are not covered under warranty.  
6.2 Customers must provide photographic evidence of any defective grocery items within the warranty period to be eligible for resolution.  

**7. Exceptions**  
7.1 Items marked as “Final Sale” or “Non-returnable” are exempt from all return policies outlined in this document.  
7.2 Gift cards and voucher codes for grocery purchases are non-returnable and non-refundable.

**End of Policy Addendum**
