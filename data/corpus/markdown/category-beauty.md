# Category Policy Addendum: Beauty

**Addendum to Customer Operations Policy: Beauty Category on Kartway Marketplace**

**1. Return Window**  
1.1. Products within the Beauty category must be returned within a **20-day** window from the date of delivery.  
1.2. Returns initiated after the **20-day** period will not be accepted.

**2. Category-Specific Condition Requirements**  
2.1. All returned Beauty items must be in **unopened** and **unused** condition to qualify for a refund.  
2.2. Items that have been opened or used will be considered non-returnable unless they fall under the exceptions listed in clause 6.

**3. Original Packaging Mandate**  
3.1. Original packaging is **mandatory** for all returns in the Beauty category.  
3.2. Items returned without their original packaging will be subject to refusal.

**4. Restocking Fees**  
4.1. A restocking fee of **15%** of the product's price will be applied to all returns within the Beauty category, except for items returned due to defects.  
4.2. The restocking fee will be deducted from the total refund amount.

**5. Hazardous or Perishable Handling Rules**  
5.1. Products classified as hazardous (e.g., aerosol sprays, certain chemicals) must be returned via ground shipping only.  
5.2. Perishable items, such as certain skincare products with expiration dates, are **non-returnable** once opened or if the return is initiated beyond the **10-day** deadline from delivery.

**6. Warranty Period**  
6.1. The warranty period for Beauty products is set at **60 days** from the date of delivery for items defective upon arrival.  
6.2. Claims for warranty service must be submitted within the **60-day** window to be eligible for a replacement or refund.

**7. Exceptions**  
7.1. Items that have been faulty upon receipt must be reported within **48 hours** of delivery to qualify for an exchange or refund without incurring a restocking fee.  
7.2. Promotional items or products marked as clearance are considered final sale and are **not returnable** under any circumstances.  

This addendum serves to clarify and specify conditions under which Beauty products can be returned and their respective handling. All support agents are expected to adhere strictly to these guidelines.
