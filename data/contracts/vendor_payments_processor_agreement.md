# Meridian Payments Processing Services Agreement

**Parties:** Kartway Commerce Pvt. Ltd. ("Kartway") and Meridian Payments Ltd. ("Meridian" or "Processor")
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2026
**Term:** Initial term of twelve (12) months from the Effective Date.

**1.1 Scope of Services**
Meridian shall provide payment processing services for all customer transactions placed through the Kartway platform, including capture, settlement, and refund disbursement on Kartway's behalf.

**2.1 Processing Fees**
Kartway shall pay Meridian a processing fee of 2.9% plus USD 0.30 per successful transaction, invoiced monthly in arrears.

**3.1 Refund Settlement Authority**
Meridian shall auto-settle any refund instruction received from Kartway's support platform up to USD 50.00 per transaction without additional written authorization. Refund instructions above USD 50.00 require a countersigned authorization from a Kartway signatory listed in Exhibit B before Meridian will release funds.

**3.2 Settlement Timing**
Refunds authorized under Clause 3.1 shall be settled to the customer's original payment method within three (3) business days of receiving Kartway's instruction.

**4.1 Term and Renewal**
This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

**5.1 Limitation of Liability**
Except for claims arising from Meridian's gross negligence or willful misconduct, Meridian's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total processing fees paid by Kartway in the one (1) month immediately preceding the claim.

**6.1 Indemnification**
Kartway shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Meridian, its officers, and employees from any claims, losses, or damages arising out of Kartway's use of the Services, including claims brought by Kartway's customers. Meridian's indemnification obligations to Kartway are limited to claims arising directly from Meridian's breach of Clause 7.1 (Data Security).

**7.1 Data Security**
Meridian shall maintain PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance for the duration of this Agreement and shall notify Kartway within seventy-two (72) hours of any confirmed data security incident affecting Kartway customer data.

**8.1 Confidentiality**
Each party shall protect the other's confidential information using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

**9.1 Governing Law**
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**10.1 Force Majeure**
Neither party shall be liable for delays or failures in performance resulting from events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, acts of government, or other force majeure events.
