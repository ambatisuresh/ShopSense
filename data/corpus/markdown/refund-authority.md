# Refund Authorisation and Escalation Matrix

**Section 4: Refund Authorization Matrix**

**4.1 Refund Approval Tiers**  
The following refund thresholds dictate the levels of authorization required for processing customer refunds. All support agents must adhere strictly to these thresholds without exception.

| Refund Amount (INR) | Approval Required                      |
|----------------------|---------------------------------------|
| Up to 2,000          | Agent auto-approval                   |
| 2,001 to 10,000      | Team Lead approval                    |
| 10,001 to 50,000     | Operations Manager approval            |
| Above 50,000         | Finance Department approval            |

**4.2 Automated System Limitation**  
4.2.1 The automated refund system is programmed to auto-approve refunds only up to the amount of 2,000 INR.  
4.2.2 Refund requests exceeding 2,000 INR must be forwarded for manual approval as outlined in Section 4.1, regardless of customer tier or sentiment.  

**4.3 Goodwill Credits**  
4.3.1 Goodwill credits may be issued to customers under the following conditions:  
- The goodwill credit amount shall not exceed 1,000 INR per order.  
- Goodwill credits are intended as a gesture of goodwill and cannot be combined with a full refund on the same order.  

4.3.2 It is prohibited to issue both a full refund and a goodwill credit for the same order. Customer service agents must ensure that if a goodwill credit is applied, no refund is processed for that order.  

**4.4 Exceptions**  
4.4.1 Any deviations from this Refund Authorization Matrix must be documented and approved in writing by the Operations Manager.  
4.4.2 Any refund request that involves fraud, abuse, or exceptional circumstances must be escalated immediately to the Finance Department for review, regardless of the amounts involved.  

All support agents must familiarize themselves with this policy to ensure compliance and maintain the integrity of our refund processes.
