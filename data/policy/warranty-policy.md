# Warranty Policy for Kartway

## 1. Introduction
This Warranty Policy outlines the responsibilities and obligations of both Kartway and the manufacturers of products sold on our platform regarding warranty claims, coverage periods, and related processes.

## 2. Warranty Responsibilities
### 2.1 Manufacturer Warranty
(a) The Manufacturer Warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship for products sold on Kartway.  
(b) The manufacturer, as specified on the product page, is responsible for honoring and managing claims under the Manufacturer Warranty.  
(c) Customers must contact the manufacturer directly to initiate any warranty claim related to the Manufacturer Warranty.

### 2.2 Kartway Warranty
(a) Kartway offers an additional warranty for select items termed the Kartway Warranty.  
(b) The Kartway Warranty covers accidental damage, missing parts, and issues not addressed by the Manufacturer Warranty.  
(c) Kartway will handle all claims related to the Kartway Warranty directly through our customer support team.

## 3. Coverage Periods
### 3.1 Coverage Durations by Product Category
(a) Electronics: 12 months from the date of purchase.  
(b) Furniture: 24 months from the date of purchase.  
(c) Clothing & Accessories: 6 months from the date of purchase.  
(d) Toys and Games: 12 months from the date of purchase.  
(e) Kitchenware: 18 months from the date of purchase.  

### 3.2 Renewal of Warranty
(a) No renewal options are available for any warranty periods upon expiration.

## 4. Warranty Voids
### 4.1 Conditions That Void a Warranty
(a) Any evidence of unauthorized repairs or modifications made to the product.  
(b) Damage resulting from misuse, negligence, or accidents including drops, spills, or extreme conditions.  
(c) Lack of proof of purchase or failure to provide the original receipt.  
(d) Failure to follow the manufacturer's instructions for use and care.  
(e) Products used for commercial purposes unless explicitly stated otherwise by the warranty terms.

## 5. Replacement vs. Repair Decision Rule
### 5.1 Decision Criteria
(a) Kartway Warranty: All decisions between replacement and repair will be made at Kartway's discretion based on:  
   (i) The severity of the issue.  
   (ii) The cost-effectiveness of repair versus replacement.  
   (iii) The age of the product.  
(b) Manufacturer Warranty: The manufacturer will determine whether to repair or replace the defective item based on their internal assessment.

## 6. Turnaround Commitments
### 6.1 Turnaround for Kartway Warranty Claims
(a) Repair: Kartway commits to completing repairs within 10 business days of receiving the product.  
(b) Replacement: Kartway commits to sending a replacement within 5 business days of the claim being approved.  

### 6.2 Turnaround for Manufacturer Warranty Claims
(a) Repair or Replacement: Manufacturers are expected to process warranty claims within 15 business days from the date of claim submission.  
(b) Kartway will provide updates to the customer regarding the status of manufacturer warranty claims every 5 business days.

## 7. Contact Information
### 7.1 Kartway Customer Support
For claims related to the Kartway Warranty, customers should contact Kartway Customer Support at support@kartway.com or call 1-800-555-0199.  
### 7.2 Manufacturer Contact
Customers must refer to the product documentation or the manufacturer's website for contact details regarding Manufacturer Warranty claims.
