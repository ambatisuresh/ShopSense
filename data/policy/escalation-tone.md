# Escalation Triggers and Customer Communication Standard

**Section 4.3: Triggers for Escalation to Human Support Interaction**

4.3.1 **Explicit Request for Human Support**  
If a customer explicitly requests to speak with a human agent during an interaction, the support agent must promptly escalate the case to a human representative without delay.

4.3.2 **Use of Abusive Language**  
Any instance of abusive or threatening language from the customer will trigger an immediate escalation. Abusive language includes, but is not limited to, profanities, hate speech, and personal attacks directed at the support agent or Kartway as an organization.

4.3.3 **Third Contact on the Same Issue**  
If a customer contacts support for the third time regarding the same issue and has not received a satisfactory resolution, the support agent is required to escalate the interaction to a human representative. This is to ensure that the customer receives a comprehensive review of their issue.

4.3.4 **Mention of Legal Action or Regulatory Complaint**  
Any mention by the customer of pursuing legal action or filing a regulatory complaint concerning their issue will trigger an immediate escalation to a human support agent for appropriate handling.

4.3.5 **Mention of Physical Harm or Product Safety**  
If the customer indicates any concern relating to physical harm or product safety, the support agent must escalate the issue to a human representative immediately. This includes any reports of injuries or potential hazards associated with a product.

4.3.6 **Refund Requests Above Automated Cap**  
In cases where a customer requests a refund that exceeds the automated cap of $50, the support agent is required to escalate the request to a human agent for review and authorization.

**Section 4.4: Tone Guidance and Liability Prohibition**  

4.4.1 **Required Tone Guidance**  
Support agents must maintain a professional and empathetic tone during all interactions. The language used must be supportive and focused on resolution while avoiding any statements that could be construed as dismissive or condescending.

4.4.2 **Prohibition on Admission of Liability**  
Support agents are strictly prohibited from apologizing in a manner that admits liability or assigns fault to Kartway or its products/services. Apologies should be limited to expressions of regret for the inconvenience experienced by the customer without implication of fault. For example, use "I'm sorry for the inconvenience this has caused you" rather than "I'm sorry our product failed."
