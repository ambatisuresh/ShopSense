# Kartway Returns and Refunds Policy

**Returns and Refunds Policy**

**2.1 Standard Return Window**  
Customers may return items purchased from Kartway within 30 days of receipt for a full refund, provided the items meet the conditions outlined in Clause 2.3.

**2.2 Category-Specific Return Windows**  
a. Electronics: Returns for electronics must be initiated within 15 days of receipt.  
b. Groceries: All grocery items are non-returnable and non-refundable.  
c. Apparel: Returns for apparel items may be initiated within 45 days of receipt.

**2.3 Condition Requirements**  
Items eligible for return must be in their original packaging and condition, including all accessories, manuals, and tags. Opened items must be in a resalable condition. Items showing evidence of use, damage, or that are incomplete will not be accepted for return.

**2.4 Return Shipping Costs**  
a. For items returned within the standard return window due to customer dissatisfaction or changes of mind, the customer is responsible for return shipping costs.  
b. For defective or incorrect items, Kartway will cover return shipping costs. Customers must provide evidence of the defect or error.  
c. In cases where the customer receives a refund for an opened item (as covered under Clause 2.5), the customer is responsible for return shipping costs regardless of the reason for the return.

**2.5 Partial Refund Schedule for Opened Items**  
a. Opened items that are eligible for return will receive a partial refund based on the following schedule:  
   i. Items opened but unused will receive a 75% refund of the original purchase price.  
   ii. Items opened and used will receive a 50% refund of the original purchase price.  
   iii. Items that are heavily used or damaged will not be refunded.

**2.6 Refund Processing Times**  
Refunds will be processed according to the following timelines based on the payment method used:  
a. Credit/Debit Card: Refunds will be processed within 5 to 7 business days from the date of approval of the return.  
b. Digital Wallet: Refunds will be processed within 24 hours from the date of approval of the return.  
c. Cash on Delivery: Refunds will be processed within 10 business days from the date of approval of the return.

**2.7 Exceptions**  
The following exceptions exist for returns and refunds:  
a. Gift cards and downloadable digital content are non-returnable and non-refundable.  
b. Items purchased during promotional sales or clearance are also non-refundable unless otherwise stated at the time of sale.  

**2.8 Return Instructions**  
Customers must initiate the return process by contacting Kartway Customer Support at support@kartway.com or by calling 1-800-555-0199 to obtain a return authorization number and further instructions. Items must be shipped back within 7 days from the date of authorization.

This Returns and Refunds Policy is effective as of October 1, 2023, and is subject to change with prior notice to customers.
