# Category Policy Addendum: Home & Kitchen

**Policy Addendum for Home & Kitchen Category on Kartway Marketplace**

**1. Return Window**  
1.1. The return window for Home & Kitchen products is 45 days from the date of delivery.  
1.2. Exceptions to the return window include:  
   1.2.1. Custom-made items, which are non-returnable.  
   1.2.2. Major appliances, which have a return window of 30 days.

**2. Condition Requirements**  
2.1. All returned Home & Kitchen items must be in "Like New" condition, defined as:  
   2.1.1. Unused and undamaged, with no visible signs of wear or tear.  
   2.1.2. Items that are missing parts or accessories will be rejected for return.

**3. Original Packaging**  
3.1. Original packaging is mandatory for all returns in the Home & Kitchen category.  
3.2. Items returned without the original packaging will incur a penalty of 20% of the item price, deducted from the refund.

**4. Restocking Fees**  
4.1. A restocking fee of 10% of the item price will be applied to all returned Home & Kitchen items, effective after the 15-day grace period beginning from the date of delivery.  
4.2. No restocking fee will be applied for items returned due to defects or shipping errors.

**5. Hazardous or Perishable Handling Rules**  
5.1. Hazardous items, such as certain cleaning agents and chemicals, are returnable only if unused and in original packaging within 30 days.  
5.2. Perishable items, including food items or kitchen gadgets with consumables, are non-returnable once opened or if the expiration date is within 30 days of purchase.

**6. Warranty Period**  
6.1. The warranty period for Home & Kitchen products is 1 year from the date of delivery, unless otherwise specified by the manufacturer.  
6.2. Major appliances come with a 2-year warranty period as stipulated by the manufacturer, which supersedes standard warranty terms.

**7. Compliance and Exceptions**  
7.1. All support agents are required to fully comply with the specific terms outlined in this addendum.  
7.2. Any exceptions to these policies must be approved by the Home & Kitchen Category Manager, Sarah Johnson, prior to processing.  

**End of Addendum**
