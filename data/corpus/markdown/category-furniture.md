# Category Policy Addendum: Furniture

**Kartway Marketplace - Furniture Category Policy Addendum**

**Policy Number: FUR-001**

**Effective Date: October 1, 2023**

**1. Return Window**  
1.1. The return window for Furniture items is strictly 45 days from the date of delivery.  
1.2. Any requests for returns submitted after the 45-day period will be denied.

**2. Category-Specific Condition Requirements**  
2.1. Furniture items must be returned in the original condition, meaning they should be free from any signs of use, wear, or damage.  
2.2. Items must include all original components, such as cushions, legs, and hardware kits.  
2.3. Any item with visible scratches or stains will not be accepted for return.

**3. Original Packaging Requirement**  
3.1. Original packaging is mandatory for the return of all Furniture items.  
3.2. If the original packaging is not available, a return will be denied regardless of the condition of the item.

**4. Restocking Fees**  
4.1. A restocking fee of 15% of the purchase price will be applied to all Furniture returns.  
4.2. This fee will be deducted from the refund amount issued to the customer.

**5. Hazardous or Perishable Handling Rules**  
5.1. Furniture items that contain hazardous materials, such as certain types of adhesives or finishes, must comply with the Hazardous Materials Policy (HMP-002) prior to return.  
5.2. Perishable materials, such as any items made from natural fibers or organic compounds, cannot be returned due to the risk of decay.

**6. Warranty Period**  
6.1. The warranty period for all Furniture items is 12 months from the date of delivery.  
6.2. Warranty claims will only be accepted for defective items that fall under the manufacturer’s warranty policy.

**Annotations**  
- This document should be reviewed periodically and updated as necessary to reflect changes in company policy or applicable law.  
- All staff are required to refer to this addendum when processing Furniture returns to ensure compliance with specific guidelines.
