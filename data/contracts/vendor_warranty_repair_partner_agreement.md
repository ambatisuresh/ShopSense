# Fixwell Repair Partners Warranty Servicing Agreement

**Parties:** Kartway Commerce Pvt. Ltd. ("Kartway") and Fixwell Repair Partners LLC ("Fixwell" or "Service Partner")
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2026
**Term:** Initial term of twenty-four (24) months from the Effective Date.

**1.1 Scope of Services**
Fixwell is appointed as an authorized service partner to handle repair and replacement claims submitted under the Kartway Warranty for Electronics, Furniture, Toys and Games, and Kitchenware product categories. This Agreement does not cover claims made under a product manufacturer's own warranty.

**2.1 Repair Turnaround**
Fixwell shall complete all approved repairs within fifteen (15) business days of receiving the product from the customer.

**2.2 Replacement Turnaround**
Where a claim is approved for replacement rather than repair, Fixwell shall use commercially reasonable efforts to ship a replacement unit to the customer as soon as practicable. No fixed turnaround time is guaranteed for replacement shipments.

**3.1 Claim Eligibility Assessment**
Fixwell shall independently assess each claim against Fixwell's standard intake criteria, which include any modification to the product's original firmware, casing, or internal components, any indication the product was used outside of ordinary household conditions, and any missing original accessories, in order to determine whether the claim is eligible for service under this Agreement.

**3.2 Repair vs. Replacement Determination**
Fixwell shall determine, in its own technical judgment, whether an eligible claim is serviced by repair or by replacement, based on the severity of the fault and the availability of parts.

**4.1 Parts Cost Threshold**
Where the estimated cost of parts required for a repair exceeds forty percent (40%) of the product's original retail price, Fixwell may decline to proceed with the repair and shall notify Kartway of the decline.

**5.1 Claim Status Reporting**
Fixwell shall provide Kartway with a status update for each open claim no less frequently than once every ten (10) business days.

**6.1 Payment Terms**
Kartway shall pay Fixwell's invoiced service fees within forty-five (45) days of invoice receipt (net-45).

**7.1 Termination**
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon ninety (90) calendar days' prior written notice to the other party.

**8.1 Confidentiality**
Each party shall keep confidential all customer data and proprietary information disclosed by the other party in connection with this Agreement, and shall use such information solely to perform its obligations hereunder.
