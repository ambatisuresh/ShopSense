# ReturnLoop Processing Services Agreement

**Parties:** Kartway Commerce Pvt. Ltd. ("Kartway") and ReturnLoop Processing Services Inc. ("ReturnLoop" or "Processor")
**Effective Date:** April 1, 2026
**Term:** Initial term of twelve (12) months from the Effective Date.

**1.1 Scope of Services**
ReturnLoop shall receive, inspect, grade, and process customer returns shipped to ReturnLoop's designated returns center on Kartway's behalf, and shall report inspection outcomes to Kartway to support refund processing.

**2.1 Return Intake Window**
ReturnLoop will accept a returned item for processing only if it arrives at ReturnLoop's designated facility within five (5) calendar days of the return authorization date issued to the customer. Items arriving after this window will be logged as late intake and are not guaranteed processing under the timelines in this Agreement.

**2.2 Condition Grading**
Upon receipt, ReturnLoop shall grade each returned item as either "Resalable" or "Not Resalable." ReturnLoop does not distinguish between opened-but-unused and opened-and-used condition states.

**2.3 Excluded Categories**
This Agreement covers returns processing for all product categories shipped to ReturnLoop's facility. ReturnLoop's standard intake process does not distinguish or reject shipments containing grocery items, gift cards, or downloadable digital content.

**3.1 Inspection Turnaround**
ReturnLoop shall complete inspection and grading of a received item within three (3) business days of receipt and shall transmit the inspection outcome to Kartway electronically no later than the end of that window.

**4.1 Liability for Mishandled Returns**
Where ReturnLoop damages, loses, or misgrades a returned item due to its own error, ReturnLoop's liability to Kartway is limited to a refund of the processing fee charged for that item and does not extend to the item's retail value.

**5.1 Data Handling**
ReturnLoop shall process customer name, address, and order data solely for the purpose of returns processing under this Agreement and shall not retain such data beyond ninety (90) days following completion of processing.

**6.1 Payment Terms**
Kartway shall pay ReturnLoop a per-item processing fee as set out in Exhibit A, invoiced monthly, payable within thirty (30) days of invoice receipt.

**7.1 Termination**
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) calendar days' prior written notice to the other party.
