# Category Policy Addendum: Toys

**Kartway Marketplace Policy Handbook: Category-Specific Policy Addendum for Toys**  
*Effective Date: October 1, 2023*

**1. Return Window**  
1.1. The return window for toys is established at 45 days from the date of delivery.  
1.2. Goods must be initiated for return by submitting a request within this 45-day period.  

**2. Category-Specific Condition Requirements**  
2.1. All returns must be in "like-new" condition, which includes:  
   2.1.1. Unused and undamaged, with no signs of wear.  
   2.1.2. All components, including accessories and instructions, must be included.  
2.2. Products with visible wear or missing parts will not be accepted for return.  

**3. Original Packaging Requirements**  
3.1. Original packaging is mandatory for all toy returns.  
3.2. Toys returned without original packaging will incur a 20% restocking fee.  
3.3. The original packaging must be in good condition, without tears or major creases.  

**4. Restocking Fees**  
4.1. A restocking fee of 5% will be applied to all returns of toys initiated after 30 days but within the allowed 45 days.  
4.2. Products returned in a damaged or unsellable condition will incur a 25% restocking fee.  

**5. Hazardous or Perishable Handling Rules**  
5.1. Toys classified as hazardous (e.g., those containing small parts intended for children under 3 years) must follow specific handling procedures:  
   5.1.1. These items must be returned with all original warning labels intact.  
   5.1.2. A return authorization number must be obtained in advance and included in the return package.  
5.2. Perishable toys (e.g., those that may include temporary materials like slime) are non-returnable after 7 days from delivery.  

**6. Warranty Period**  
6.1. The warranty period for toys is 90 days from the date of delivery.  
6.2. Warranty claims must include proof of purchase and can only be processed if the product was used in accordance with the manufacturer's guidelines.  

**7. Exceptions**  
7.1. Custom or personalized toys are non-returnable unless they are defective or damaged upon delivery.  
7.2. Limited edition toys may have different handling rules and will be clearly marked on the product page. 

All customer support agents must ensure that these category-specific rules are communicated clearly and adhered to in every interaction regarding toys on the Kartway marketplace.
